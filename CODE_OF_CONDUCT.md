# Community code of conduct

XNET~ Harness is built through careful technical disagreement. Contributors and maintainers must make the project a place where people can participate without harassment or intimidation.

## Expected conduct

- Be respectful, specific and patient.
- Critique claims, interfaces and evidence rather than people.
- State uncertainty and correct mistakes openly.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution and the licenses of other projects.
- Keep security reports and personal information in the appropriate private channel.
- Help make discussions accessible to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate exposure of secrets, sustained disruption, impersonation and knowingly false attribution are not acceptable. Retaliation against a person who raises a good-faith concern is also unacceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and project-run community spaces. It also applies when someone formally represents the project in another space.

## Reporting and enforcement

For an urgent GitHub safety concern, use [GitHub's abuse reporting process](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse). For project moderation, open a minimal issue asking for private maintainer contact without publishing sensitive details. Security vulnerabilities belong in the private channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers will review available evidence, protect the reporter's privacy as far as practical and choose a response proportionate to the conduct. Responses may include a clarification, warning, content removal, temporary participation limit or permanent ban. A maintainer who is directly involved in a report should not make the enforcement decision when another uninvolved maintainer is available.

Anyone asked to stop unacceptable behavior is expected to comply promptly.
